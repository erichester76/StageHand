# Stripe Connect Evaluation Trigger

Use this document as a reminder, not as an active decision.

Do not introduce Stripe Connect until at least one of these becomes true:

- StageHand needs to collect funds on behalf of bands
- StageHand needs platform fees deducted automatically
- StageHand needs automated downstream payouts to multiple members
- StageHand needs a platform-controlled merchant experience instead of band-owned payment links

Until then, prefer simpler hosted payment flows and band-owned payment destinations.
